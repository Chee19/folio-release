Privacy notice

Last updated 28 October 2026

This notice covers this website and the form you fill in to download Folio. The Folio app itself keeps your portfolio on your own computer.

Who we are

Folio is a personal project by Delphi. You can reach Delphi at hello@delphi-engine.com.

What we collect

When you fill in the download form, we receive:

  • your name and email address
  • whether you ticked the newsletter and marketing box
  • which installer you downloaded, and when

You need to give your name and email to download Folio from this site. The newsletter and marketing emails are optional.

We don’t use cookies or analytics. Your browser keeps a small note that you’ve signed up, so we don’t ask again. It stays on your computer.

Why we use it

We use your details only for these purposes:

  • Surveys and updates, for everyone who signs up. We send the occasional feedback survey and tell you about new versions and important fixes, matched to the installer you chose. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in running a useful beta and learning what to improve, which you’d expect when you sign up for one.
  • Marketing and the newsletter, only if you ticked the box. We do this with your consent.

We don’t sell your details or share them for anyone else’s use.

Who handles it for us

  • Web3Forms, run by Web3Creative, receives the form and emails it to us. It keeps a copy for a limited time, and its spam checks can see your email and IP address.
  • Our email provider stores the sign-up emails in our inbox.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the installers, and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

Some of these services are based outside the UK, including in India and the United States. When your details leave the UK, we rely on the safeguards in each provider’s data processing terms. Email us if you’d like a copy.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while the beta runs. If you ticked the newsletter and marketing box, we keep them until you unsubscribe. Otherwise, we delete them within six months of the beta ending. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can object at any time to us using your details to run the beta. Reply “unsubscribe” to any email from us, or email hello@delphi-engine.com, and we’ll stop.

You can also withdraw your newsletter and marketing consent the same way, as easily as you gave it, and ask us to:

  • send you a copy of the details we hold about you
  • correct them
  • delete them
  • stop using them while a concern is sorted out
  • send them to you or someone else in a common format

We’ll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your details, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If anything here changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Folio is not financial advice.
